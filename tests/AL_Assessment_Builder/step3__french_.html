My paper

Q1. Year 7 Module 1 Listening 1

This task tests the understanding of single lexical items in the context of likes. Pupils write down the correct letter. There are two distractors. Each statement will be heard twice.


Audioscript

1 Moi, les jeux vidéo.

2 J’adore la danse.

3 Les araignées!

4 Moi, le cinéma.

5 J’aime les chiens.

6 Moi, j’adore la musique.


Answers

1 D    2 G    3 A     4 F     5 B     6 E

Q2. Year 7 Module 1 Reading 1

This task tests the understanding of single lexical items in the context of school equipment. Pupils write down the correct percentage. There is one distractor.


Answers

1 13%      2 2%     3 22%     4 5%     5 34%     6 6%

Q3. Year 7 Module 1 Writing 1

This task tests the ability to write single words with support in the context of leisure activities. The first letter of each word is given and the words are supplied in random order.


Answers

1 natation     2 boules     3 gymnastique     4 équitation     5 promenades

Q4. Year 7 Module 1 Listening 2

This task tests the understanding of familiar statements in the context of descriptions. Pupils write down the correct letter. There is one distractor. Each statement will be heard twice.


Audioscript

1 Elle a les cheveux mi-longs, blonds et raides.

2 Il a les cheveux longs, noirs et raides.

3 Elle a les cheveux courts, noirs et raides.

4 Il a les cheveux mi-longs, blonds et frisés.

5 Elle a les cheveux longs, noirs et frisés.

6 Il a les cheveux courts, blonds et raides.


Answers

1 E    2 A    3 G    4 C    5 F     6 H

Q5. Year 7 Module 1 Reading 2

This task tests the understanding of sentences using familiar vocabulary in the context of likes and dislikes. Pupils write the correct name. There is one distractor.


Answers

1 Joachim     2 Khalida     3 Alexandre     4 Benasa     5 Julie     6 Romain

Q6. Year 7 Module 1 Reading 3

This task tests the understanding of short sentences using familiar vocabulary in the context of descriptions. Pupils write the correct letter. There is one distractor.


Answers

1 C     2 H     3 A     4 D      5 B     6 G

Q7. Year 7 Module 1 Writing 2

Pupils write individual words from memory in the context of everyday objects.


Answers

1 une trousse     2 une clé USB     3 des chips     4 une gourde     5 un miroir     6 un portemonnaie

Q8. Year 7 Module 1 Writing 3

This task tests the ability to write a short paragraph with support in the context of descriptions.
