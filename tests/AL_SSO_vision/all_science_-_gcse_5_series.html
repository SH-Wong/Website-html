Playlist thinking.

• We know usage is minimal. (2017 Q1: ~ 0.22%.)

• We know that playlist function is not optimal.

• There is a pattern of spike in September, then drop of quickly in Oct, and level out in Jan to July. (Suggest inital interest but lack of longer term usefulness? Or are the planning all done in Sep?)

• But there are users (0.22% equate to 39,782 page views)

• And playlist is integrated throughout AT features.


• There is then 2 possible future for playlist: Improve / remove.

• Either way we can (?) bypass exposing playlist further at this point, as we ether want to wind it down, or we don't need to promote it until it can be improved.
