The following content is an early release of topic 7A from Exploring Science: Working Scientifically (Year 7).


Each unit is split into five lessons. You can choose either to view all the resources available for this unit or follow one of the suggested differentiated routes.


Click on the unit titles below to start viewing the content available for each route.


The Planner collates all the resources from Presentation, Activity Pack, Assessment Pack and the Teacher and Technician Planning Pack, and presents them in differentiated lesson plans. If you choose not to purchase one of the packs then those resources will appear in grey in the planner and you will not be able to access them.

7A Cells, tissues, organs & systems (All the content)  

This unit starts by reminding students about the features of organisms, and then looks at organs, tissues and cells. These ideas are then built back up in order to look at organs once again, in the context of organ systems. Throughout the unit, students are encouraged to compare what we know about what organisms are made of today compared to what people believed in the past. The theme of Ancient Egypt helps to thread these ideas together.
