7A Life Processes



This Securing route contains lesson plans and resources for students who are meeting the standard defined by the National Curriculum.


 

Please note in that if you have not purchased any or some of the following ActiveTeach download packs for Exploring Science: Activity Pack; Assessment Pack and Teacher and Technician Planning Pack; then the resources linked to those packs will not be available for you.



Year 7 is divided into 12 Units. Each Unit is divided into 5 Topics. Each Topic is then arranged into Starters, Exploring, Explaining, Plenaries and Homework Tasks.


This is the same Year 7 route whether you are following the 2-year, 2.5-year or 3-year version of the course.
