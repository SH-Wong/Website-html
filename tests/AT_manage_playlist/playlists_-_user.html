Playlists

Playlists provide an easy way to organise your resources. You can use it to prepare class presentations, plan your lessons, and share your favourite resources with others.


Using 'My Playlists'

• Create a new playlist: Click on the 'New Playlist' button. A new list

   item will appear, give it and name and click the  button to create

   the playlist.

• Opening a playlist: Click the name of a playlist to open it. Once opened,

   you can see the playlist content on the right hand side panel.

• Editing a playlist: Open the playlist you would like to edit, you can now

   renaming your playlist by clicking  at the top of the right hand side

   panel. Add resources using the 'Add resource', 'upload resource' and

   'Add weblink' buttons. And remove unwanted resources from your

   playlist by clicking .


Organising your playlists

• Create a folder: Click on the 'New Folder' button. A new folder will

   appear in the tree structure, give it and name and click the  button to

   create the folder.

• Sharing your playlists: Select the playlist you would like to share by

   clicking on the check box next to its name. Then click the 'Share' button

   above the tree structure. In the share modal, select the individual(s) you

   would like to share with, then click the 'Share' button to confirm.


Pearson Playlists

Pearson playlists are pre-populated lists supplied with your product subscriptions, and could be used as a basis for your own lesson plans!


• Editing a Pearson Playlist: Pearson Playlists forms the default template for your product subscriptions, to edit them, you must first make a copy to 'My Playlist'. To copy a Pearson Playlist, open the playlist, then click on the 'Edit Playlist' button on the right hand side panel. In the Edit Playlist modal, you can then rename and choose where to save your new editable playlist!
