 An Introduction to Energy Audits: opportunities to look for and how to find them

<

This session is brought to you in collaboration with Vilnis Vesma







This recorded training session is an introduction to running a successful energy audit to identify and prioritize savings in your business. It is aimed at those just starting their energy management journey, or looking to refresh their personal skills. The session outlines guidance on Energy Auditing and the importance of analysing consumption data. Vilnis Vesma will take you through the process of energy auditing from two angles; firstly, looking for opportunities and their categorization and secondly, detailing the different approaches you can take to carry out an energy audit.



Why are energy audits important?

• Data collection enables you to gain a better understanding of your energy consumption and

   identify problems and areas for improvement.

• They can provide a chance to generate a significant impact: saving energy and money.

• They can also add benefits; such as extended operating life of the equipment.

 


Agenda:

• Physical energy audits: methodologies, depth and thoroughness

• The analysis of energy consumption profiles

• Relating consumption to driving factors (electricity usage VS. production outputs)

• Setting priorities for energy management

• Covering all conditions that facilities can operate in looking at buildings and processes.

• Quantifying opportunities: Benchmarking (comparison) vs. Risk of Loss (Consumption cost X Risk

   Factor)

 


Key Takeaways:

• There are three opportunity categories for auditing: technical; human factors & procedural

• Desktop analysis should be done prior to an energy audit to gain an idea of what to expect

• There are three types of audit approaches: checklist based, product-led and opportunity-led.

 

About Vilnis:
