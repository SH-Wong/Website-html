Welcome to the Manufacture 2030 platform (M2030)


To get started we recommend that you:

    •  Manage members: Check the right colleagues have access to M2030, and invite any relevant colleagues who don’t have access.


For each license you have access to*:

    1. Select customers: to confirm who you are currently supplying and your participation in their supply chain program.

    2. Review: Resource and units. To select your preferred units of measure.

    3. Explore: Actions. For improvement ideas and start building your: Action plan.

    4. Enter: Measurements. Submit your annual data, based on your business' and customers' requests.


* If you have access to multiple licenses, you’ll see a selector on the right of the top navigation bar.

For more guidance on how to get started, visit our Quick Start guide.
