Welcome to Manufacture 2030


To get started we recommend that you:

    •  Manage members: to check that the right colleagues have access, and invite others to join.


Then for each of your license *:

    1. Enter: Measurements. Select customers to participate in their supply chain programs, and submit your annual data based on requests from your

        business and customers.

    2. Create an: Action plan. Create improvement actions, set target, and record savings to help manage your improvement plan, and empower reports to

        help long term planning. For more improvement ideas visit the: Actions library


For more guidance on how to get started, visit our Quick Start guide.


* If you have access to multiple licenses, you’ll see a selector on the right of the top navigation bar.
