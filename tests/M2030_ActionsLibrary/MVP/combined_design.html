Action templates are potential projects that can be added to your Action plan.


The templates can be customised when added to your Action Plan. You can add your own project name; notes; potential costs and financial & environment benefits.


Each time you or a colleague create a new action, a template is added to your library, allowing both you and colleagues to repeat the action and share all associated data.
