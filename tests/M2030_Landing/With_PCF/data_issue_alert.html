Guide to the PCF builder



To build a cradle to gate Product Carbon Footprint:

1. Create a product profile in: Products & PCF builder

2. Launch the PCF builder within the product profile

3. Follow each step within the PCF builder and provide all

    relevant data for your product to calculate the PCF.
