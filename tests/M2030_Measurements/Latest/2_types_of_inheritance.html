Scenario 1: Existing supplier - year roll over from 2021 to 2022.

• Submitted data for 2020 (during 2021)

• Current year rolled over to 2022

• No data submitted yet in 2021

Idea behind 2 type of inheritance

1. To apply the buyer selection from current period -1 to selection in current period.

2. As reporting period roll over, inherit the buyer selection from (old) current period, to (new) current period.

     Option 1

     1.1: Inherit if buyer is selected in -1, and % is available in -1 or -2

     1.2: Inherit if buyer is selected in -1 but not -2 (regardless of % available)

        * Only reason buyer is not selected in -1 is if they were removed on purpose

        * Selection in -1 but not -2 indicates a new facility only started supplying

          in 2022


     Option 2

     • Inherit if buyer is selected in -1 (regardless of other factors)

        X Result in buyer selection to inherit perpetually.
