Total scope 1&2 CO2e:



Scope 1 emissions are emissions from sources that are owned or controlled by your operation.


If you know your total scope 1 emissions from a LCA or other type of emissions audit, you can uncheck ‘Calculate from Scope 1 segments’ and manually enter your value.


If ‘Calculate from Scope 1 segments’ is checked, your Total scope 1 emissions will be calculated as a sum of its segments:

• Scope 1 Fugitive emissions CO2e

• Scope 1 Mobile combustion CO2e

• Scope 1 CO2e emissions from Energy 

  (stationary combustion)



Scope 2 emissions are emissions that results from the activities of the your operation, but occur at sources owned or controlled by another entity.


If you know your total scope 2 emissions from a LCA or other type of emissions audit, you can uncheck ‘Estimate from Energy values’ and manually enter your value.


If ‘Calculate from segment values’ is checked, your Total scope 2 value will be estimated based on the energy consumption values you have submitted for:

• Electricity (standard)

• Electricity (renewable purchased)

• Purchased cooling

• Purchased heat

• Purchased steam



Scope 1 Other direct GHG emissions are any direct emissions other then fugitive emissions and mobile combustion. Typically from fuel usage in onsite energy generation.


If you know this value from a LCA or other type of emissions audit, you can uncheck ‘Estimate from Energy values’ and manually enter your value.


If ‘Estimate from Energy values’ is checked, this will be estimates based on the energy consumption values you have submitted in the ‘Energy’ section.
