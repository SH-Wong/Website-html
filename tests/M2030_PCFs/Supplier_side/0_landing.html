All data gathered and calculated in M2030 are private to your organization by default.


Participating in customers’ supply-chain programs Allocated environmental data are only shared with specific customers when you confirm participation by selecting a customer in: Measurements


PCF data are only shared with customers when you explicitly share specific PCFs, or respond to a request.


In those cases, only PCF emissions intensity are shared, input data used to calculate PCFs remain private to your organization.


Business sensitive data such as raw energy value or financial data will not be shared in any case; and are always kept secured to your organization.
