Process emissions include emissions from chemical transformation of raw materials and fugitive emissions.


These emission can be determined by a variety of methods including:

• a CEMS

• a mass balance approach

• or site-specific or default emission factors.


Chemical transformation of raw materials often releases greenhouse gases such as CO2, CH4, and N2O. Example processes include:

• iron and steel production

• cement production

• nitric acid production, and others.
