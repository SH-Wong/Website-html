* PCF measurement unit

Please provide a physical measurement for a unit of this product



Feedback being considered:

3 of 3 tested suppliers find ‘PCF measurement unit’ a bit confusing:

• Not initially clear what it means?

• Is it the same as the ‘Product measurement unit’?

• Hint of confusing it with measuring the TOTAL physical measurement if all of a product.

Consider solution


1. Link the selection of prod.


So if a compatible unit is selected at product level, it is auto selected at the PCF level (by default), and at PCF level just to clarify the the need for a value.



2. Rename to something like:

Product physical measurement. Then explain it is for the Declared Unit.


The ‘unit’ and similarity of the phrase to product level unit contribute to the confusion.

If the data is ultimately about a physical attribute of the product, just call things what they are.

Suggested improvement 1

Rename the PCF measurement unit label

To clarify what is being asked for

The measurement of a physical property of your product is required to set a declared unit for PCF calculations.


1. Choose a unit appropriate for your product.

    (e.g. ‘kg’ of steel bracket, or ‘L’ of a chemical)


2. Measure one unit of your product, and provide

    the value. (e.g. steel bracket is ‘1.5’ kg.)
