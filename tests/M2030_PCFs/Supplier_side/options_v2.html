1.3 Calculations


For each material:


A. Select emission intensity factors:

= Select 1+ transportation mode.


B. Calculate transport activity for each transport mode:

= Mass of material * Amount of material used in this product * distance traveled

   (result in t-km)


C. Calculate emission for each transport mode:

= Intensity factor * transport activity (t-km)


D. Estimate transshipment/storage emissions between transport modes:

= For each part of transport modes: Material mass * default transshipment emission factors


E. Sum results of C and D
