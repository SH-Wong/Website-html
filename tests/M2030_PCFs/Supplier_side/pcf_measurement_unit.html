Declared unit is the unit of analysis for the PCF,

and sets the reference for the data input. (Amount of material, energy…etc).


Set the declared unit by measuring a physical property of one unit of your product:


1. Choose a unit appropriate for your product.

    (e.g. ‘kg’ of steel bracket, or ‘L’ of a chemical)


2. Measure one unit of your product, and provide

    the value. (e.g. 1 piece of steel bracket

    measured to be: ‘1 kg’.)

1. Change PCF measurement unit to PCF declared unit.

- To call declared unit what it is

- To differentiate from Product measurement unit.

- Prompt use of tooltip


2. Insert the Product measurement unit (e.g. Piece) into the help text:

Provide a physical measurement for one [UNIT] of this product


3. Tweak tooltip to explain what Declared unit actually is for.
