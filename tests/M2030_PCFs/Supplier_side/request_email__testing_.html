Respond to PCF requests: Step by step

1. Understanding requests

     - How to understand a buyer’s category?


2. Determine relevance. Did you supply those products to the customer?


3. Determine what if you are ready to respond

     - Options to respond to product level / category level requests.

     - Do you have PCFs matching the buyer’s Product / category definition?

     - If not build PCF

     - If so ensure they are in M2030 and respond.


4. Attached PCFs to responses

    - Select PCF that matches the requirements

    - Match PCF to the customer’s product ID if viable.

   - Record how many are supplied to customer in the requested year


5. Confirm and submit response.
