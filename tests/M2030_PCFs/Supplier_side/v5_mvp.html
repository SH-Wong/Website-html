PCF requests can be at a Product or a Category level.


Product level request

Customer requested for PCFs of an individual Product SKU.


Category level requests

Customer requested for PCFs that represents multiple products, grouped by a customer’s category. This can be fulfilled in multiple way. To learn more, please visit: Category level PCF guide



Requests are defined by customers based on their internal product / category names.


To identify the right PCFs to respond with, review the request definitions in ‘View / respond’ page for each request.

All data gathered and calculated in M2030 are private to your organization by default.


PCF data are only shared with customers when you explicitly share specific PCFs, or respond to a request.


In those cases, only PCF emissions intensity are shared, input data used to calculate PCFs remain private to your organization.
