Welcome to the Manufacture 2030 platform (M2030)


To get started, add new licences in the Manage licences page. (Required)

     •  Each licence can be linked to a reporting entity in your business, and doing  so will unlock the full features

         in the platform.


Then we recommend that you:

    1. Manage members: Check the right colleagues have access to M2030, and invite any who don’t.


For each licence you have access to:

    2. Select customers you supply to confirm who you share data with.

    3. Edit Resource and units to select your preferred measurements and units.

    4. Explore the Actions database to start building to your action plan.

    5. Enter you annual Measurements data, based on your business’s and customers’ requests.
