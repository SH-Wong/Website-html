Pro-rata savings

• Calculate monthly savings of actions, by dividing the annual savings values by 12.

• Then for each month within a reporting period:

   • Calculate the monthly aggregated savings,

      • including savings from actions that are completed/targeted in or before the month.

      • Then within the reporting period, aggregated savings from month to month.


Purpose of the savings report?


Savings durations

• TBC how long does savings accumulate for?

  • For example if an action is completed in June. Does:

     1. Savings accumulate until end of period (assume Dec, result in 6 months savings accounted for, but contained within period. Assume new baseline

        at beginning of a period.)

     2. Or accumulate for 12 months (Until June of next period, result in 12 months savings, but cross periods. Assume next baseline after 12 months….

        per action.)

     - Or implement savings duration? (Allow actions to have different duration of savings, until the new state become baseline.)
