How to add members?

To add members, an Okta admin from your organisation must give them permission to access M2030 via Okta.


Once they have access, please assign licences to new members to allow them to contribute.

• Hide ‘Add member’

• Optional: Add help text directing them to Okta

1. if email and name exists, Log in.

     b) present welcome form to capture name and Job title.
